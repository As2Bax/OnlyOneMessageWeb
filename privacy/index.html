Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 13, 2025

By inviting OnlyOneMessage or using its functionality, you agree to our Privacy Policy. As mentioned in our Terms of Service, updates to this can happen at any time, including additions, removals, and edits.

Information We Collect

For OnlyOneMessage to function correctly, we collect the following data:

  • Guild name and ID – Used for identification.
  • Specific Channel IDs – Used to find and detect messages in a specified channel.
  • Member IDs – Stored temporarily for up to 1 month, used for Automatic Unblacklist configuration.
  • Configurations – Includes logging channel ID, configuration toggles, and more.
  • Usage Telemetry – Temporary, anonymous data about how OnlyOneMessage features are used. Used to improve the bot & add insight.

What We Do With Your Data

Collected data is only used to provide core bot functionality such as automatic blacklisting and custom server settings. We do not share your data with any third parties under any circumstances.

Data Removal

You can remove your data at any time by using the /revert command or by removing the bot from your server. Both actions will permanently delete all stored data associated with your server from our database.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding your data or this policy, please reach out via our support server and create a ticket.
